RE5 Control Plus - Based on RE3Controls

Resident Evil 5 Gold Edition (Steam 1.2.0, 32-bit re5dx9.exe).
Independent community fork of RE3Controls v1.9, formerly RE5Plus.
Version: 1.9.4-beta.1.
Source: https://github.com/tfz96/RE5-Control-Plus
Releases: https://github.com/tfz96/RE5-Control-Plus/releases

FEATURES
Retains RE3Controls free camera and controls, movement while aiming/reloading,
FOV adjustment and yellow-filter removal.
Adds configurable FPS limiting (packaged at 144 FPS), mouse-wheel gun cycling,
and optional health/ammo/no-reload/grenade helpers. Trainer options default OFF.
Includes a Chinese-commented INI, a full Chinese configuration guide and English instructions.

INSTALL
Close the game. Back up existing xinput1_3.dll and RE3Controls.ini.
Extract the release and place those two files next to re5dx9.exe.
Use Variable frame rate in the game's options.
For upgrades, replace the DLL and keep your existing INI.
Restore backed-up files to uninstall.
Do not load another RE3Controls DLL or RE5Fix at the same time.

VALIDATION
The preceding RE5Plus.3 build was used by the owner to complete the main campaign.
This beta removes an inactive heat option and updates documentation.
The rebuilt beta has not received a new in-game regression pass.
A LAN room-discovery issue was reported; its relationship to this mod is unconfirmed.
Use optional trainer settings in solo play or private co-op with everyone's agreement.
